# Braden: happy, confident, healthy

A practical parenting library for **15–24 months**, built around Braden's love of books, painting, water, and active play. Prepared September 18, 2026.

## Start here

1. Read the [15-month plan](months/15-months.md). Pick one invitation to play; repetition is welcome.
2. Use the [family routine](guides/routine.md) to fit it around daycare.
3. Set up the [playroom](guides/playroom.md) and follow the activity safety notes.
4. Keep observations in copies of the [templates](templates/README.md), preferably in the ignored `private/` folder.

Success means shared enjoyment, chances to explore, and a caregiver who responds. Finishing activities or reaching skills early is not the goal. No plan can guarantee a particular personality or lifelong health.

## What “science-based” means here

The foundation uses WHO guidelines, CDC developmental guidance, and American Academy of Pediatrics parent resources. The [evidence register](evidence/README.md) identifies what each source supports. Specific games and their placement in a particular month are practical adaptations, not experimentally proven prescriptions. CDC provides checkpoints at **15, 18, and 24 months** within this period, not a separate standard for each intervening month.

This is general parenting education, not Braden's medical record or an individualized treatment plan. Discuss development or health concerns with his clinician; do not wait for the next planned checkpoint if a skill is lost or a concern arises. US screening guidance is labeled; local schedules may differ.

## Keeping this useful

Spend a few minutes each week recording one joy, one emerging skill, and one question. At the next month, reuse favorites and change only what helps. Review sources when medical guidance changes; they are not automatically monitored.

The repository is plain Markdown: no installation or subscription is needed. Open this README in a Markdown viewer or editor. Filled health notes belong in `private/`, which Git ignores; that exclusion is not encryption or a backup. No health records have been entered. Remote hosting is not configured yet.
