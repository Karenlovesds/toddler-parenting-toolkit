# QIQI (奇奇): example customization

This example uses a nickname and intentionally leaves out identifying, medical, and location details.

## What we know

- QIQI is a **very active toddler** who benefits from frequent safe movement and outdoor exploration across the day.
- Weekdays include daycare, so home activities stay short and connection comes first after pickup.
- Favorite interests are books, painting, water play, and active movement.
- The family wants to nurture confidence, happiness, and health without pressure to perform.
- The home has room for active play.

## How the toolkit adapts

- Offer movement throughout the day and keep a clear indoor route for carrying, rolling, pushing, and walking games.
- Alternate energetic play with books, music, cuddles, or simple art so the child can shift between activity levels.
- Use water and painting as motivating ways to explore cause and effect, while following the close-supervision rules.
- After childcare or a busy day, reconnect and observe energy before adding a planned activity. A favorite book may be a better choice than a new challenge.
- Offer two acceptable choices and notice effort, communication, and recovery from frustration.
- Keep exact sleep, meal, growth, and health records in a private local copy, not this public example.

This is one family example, not a recommended personality type or schedule. “Active” describes a current pattern and should not become a label that limits the child's quieter interests or need for rest.
