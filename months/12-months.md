# 12 months — Connect, contain, and move

[Home](../README.md) · [Daily rhythm](../guides/routine.md) · [Activity instructions](../guides/activities.md)

**Checkpoint month:** use the [full CDC 1-year checklist](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/1-year.html), alongside clinician advice.

## Parenting focus

Create safe space for exploration and respond to looks, gestures, sounds, and attempts to communicate. Repetition is useful and welcome.

## Choose one invitation at a time

- **STEAM:** Put one large block into a wide cup or container, take it out, and let the child try.
- **Art:** Make a water mark with a large brush on a washable surface while supervising closely.
- **Reading and language:** Talk about one picture in a sturdy book; point, name, and pause.
- **Sports and movement:** Place a favorite object within safe reach for crawling, cruising, or walking according to current ability. Do not force a position or skill.
- **Music:** Play pat-a-cake or copy one clap, tap, or sound.
- **Confidence and connection:** Respond when the child asks with a look, gesture, sound, or word: “You showed me. Here it is.”

These are options across the month, not a daily checklist. Reconnect after childcare or separation and offer an activity only if the child is interested.

## Adjust to your child

Reduce the materials, steady an object, or participate while seated. Add one small variation when the child is engaged. Stop for tiredness, distress, refusal, or unsafe mouthing. Follow the [material, water, and movement safety notes](../guides/playroom.md).

## Health and observations

Review the full one-year checklist and prepare for the 12-month well visit according to local guidance. Ask about feeding transitions, cup practice, teeth, growth, vaccinations, sleep, and any developmental concerns. Breastfeeding can continue as desired; feeding advice should reflect the child's needs and clinician guidance. Review the [food guide](../guides/food-safety.md).

**Reflection:** How does the child invite you into play or ask for help?

Record a natural example in the [observation template](../templates/milestone-observation.md). Missing milestones, loss of skills, or any concern should prompt a clinician discussion.

## Evidence and limits

[CDC 1-year milestones](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/1-year.html), [WHO responsive care](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/97892400020986), and [AAP play guidance](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/family-life/power-of-play/Pages/the-power-of-play-how-fun-and-games-help-children-thrive.aspx) support the broad approach. These particular games are practical adaptations, not a validated age-specific program.
