# 13 months — Repeat and discover

[Home](../README.md) · [Daily rhythm](../guides/routine.md) · [Activity instructions](../guides/activities.md)

**Activity theme only:** there is no separate CDC checklist for this month. Use the [12- and 15-month checkpoints](../guides/milestones.md) and raise concerns whenever they arise.

## Parenting focus

Repeat familiar actions and let the child explore cause and effect without turning play into a test.

## Choose one invitation at a time

- **STEAM:** Drop a large block into a container, tip it out, and try again.
- **Art:** Offer one large age-appropriate drawing tool and secured paper.
- **Reading and language:** Reread a favorite page and name what catches the child's attention.
- **Sports and movement:** Roll a soft ball or invite movement between two safe, stable surfaces based on current ability.
- **Music:** Sing a familiar verse and pause for a look, movement, or sound.
- **Confidence and connection:** Let the child choose between two safe objects and accept a reach or look as an answer.

These are options across the month, not a daily checklist. Reconnect after childcare or separation and offer an activity only if the child is interested.

## Adjust to your child

Participate at floor level, bring objects within reach, and help only as much as needed. Do not rush walking or any other skill. Follow the [safety notes](../guides/playroom.md).

## Health and observations

Notice how food textures, cup practice, sleep, and new mobility are changing. Recheck reachable hazards whenever the child begins moving in a new way.

**Reflection:** What action does the child enjoy repeating, and what seems to change each time?

## Evidence and limits

[WHO responsive care](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/97892400020986) and [AAP play guidance](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/family-life/power-of-play/Pages/the-power-of-play-how-fun-and-games-help-children-thrive.aspx) support the broad approach. This month's activity grouping is a practical adaptation, not a milestone standard.
